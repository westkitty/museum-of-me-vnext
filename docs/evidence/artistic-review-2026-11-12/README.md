Notes:

1. The two "archive shelves" are plain tall slabs with no visible shelving or books, so they read as partitions.
2. The dark-grey and black document table does not match the light-wood desk and shelves.
3. The parts read as separate furniture standing side by side, not as one research station.
4. The **logging computer floats** at desk height beyond the desk's edge. It is not resting on the desk or the floor.
5. The telescope reads as a pipe on a trestle. Its white drum appears **detached above the tube**.
6. The star-chart shelf is a blank slab.

## What would change these verdicts

These are the owner's design choices, so the templates were not changed here:

- Put the computer on the desk surface. This needs a surface anchor or the desk-top height.
- Use library models with visible shelving, or add book and chart dressing.
- Unify the material palette across the parts.
- Fix or replace the telescope model.

After that, re-capture and review again in an interior bay.
