# E02 Drakken source basis

E02 follows the preserved archaeology `PROJECT_LEDGER.csv` record for P002 and
the current `The-Drakken-Terraforming-Laboratory/docs/CANON_BOUNDARIES.md`.
Together they establish a 37-entry frame: one Egg, 35 strains in five
archetypes (seven per archetype), and one Mother.

The source-supported named references available to this museum are process
functions: Fault-Tongue (geological fracture), Cloudmaw (hydrology), Gorevault
(collection through prepared feedstock), and Ringthroat (downstream sky/orbital
construction). They are not displayed as biological archetypes. The fifth E02
station is explicitly a museum taxonomy abstraction because the preserved
sources do not name or describe the remaining representatives. Its glyphs are
diagrammatic, not morphology claims.
